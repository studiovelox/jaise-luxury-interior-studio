Portfolio Chapter I

Apartments & Penthouses

“Where curated stillness meets the vertical horizon of the city.”

Our urban residences transform high-rise floor plates into intimate European salons. By pairing bookmatched Italian Calacatta marble with fluted walnut panelling, custom brass ironmongery, and museum-grade lighting, each apartment becomes a private gallery suspended above the metropolis.

Worli Sea Face, Mumbai Altamount Road Mayfair, London Downtown Dubai
Portfolio Chapter II

Farmhouses & Villas

“Indoor-outdoor pavilions where raw limestone, Burmese teak, and monsoon breezes converge.”

Designed for unhurried generational living, our country estates and coastal villas celebrate tactile honesty. Courtyards framed by colonnades, hand-troweled lime plaster walls, bespoke rattan and bouclé lounging, and sunken conversation pits dissolve the boundary between architecture and landscape.

Chhatarpur Farms, New Delhi Awas, Alibaug Assagao, Goa Coonoor
Portfolio Chapter III

Office Interiors

“Executive sanctuaries engineered for clarity of thought and understated authority.”

Departing from sterile corporate convention, our private family offices, law chambers, and headquarters embrace residential refinement. Acoustic leather wall panelling, custom bronze and walnut boardroom tables, private art galleries, and hospitality-grade lounges define a new paradigm of leadership space.

BKC, Mumbai Nariman Point DIFC, Dubai Connaught Place, Delhi
